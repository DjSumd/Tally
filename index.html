How this holds your data

  • Pick your Finance folder once as the workspace. The app keeps tally-data.json there as the single source of truth, and reads and writes your invoice PDFs straight from it. No copies live inside the browser.
  • Each row's Invoice file shows green when its own invoice is attached, amber when it only points at a combined scan or a file shared with another row, and red dashed when nothing is attached. Click it to preview, or to attach.
  • To attach: drag a PDF onto the row, click the red Attach, or drop it into the add/edit form. The file is copied into the Invoices subfolder and renamed in your usual pattern, e.g. Invoice - 2026-05-05 - Harry Oliff - Arts Day Sound Tech ($500) (INV-4034, PO538668).pdf.
  • Find invoice files in folder scans the Finance folder for PDFs that aren't linked yet and suggests matches by invoice number, PO and amount. You confirm each link.
  • Grant the folder access when Chrome asks (once per session). With autosave on, every change is written to tally-data.json a second later; the pill shows the time of the last save.
  • Start new year first saves an archive copy (e.g. tally-2026.json) into the Finance folder, then clears invoices, ledger and allocations. Committed POs are carried into the new year.
