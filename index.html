How this holds your data

  • Pick your Finance folder once as the workspace. The app keeps tally-data.json there as the single source of truth, and reads and writes your invoice PDFs straight from it. No copies live inside the browser.
  • Each invoice row has a File cell: Open opens the linked PDF, Attach copies a new PDF into the Invoices subfolder and links it. The 2026 entries are already linked to the files we renamed.
  • Grant the folder access when Chrome asks (once per session). The pill up top says unsaved until you Save. Do that before closing.
